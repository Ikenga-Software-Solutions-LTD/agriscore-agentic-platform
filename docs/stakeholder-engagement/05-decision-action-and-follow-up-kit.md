## 5. Minimum meeting outputs

The meeting is not complete until the facilitator can state the answers below.

| Question | Required answer |
|---|---|
| What was agreed? | A concise list of confirmed decisions, clearly distinguished from informal views. |
| What was not agreed? | Unresolved issues, constraints, referrals, and questions requiring evidence. |
| Who does what next? | Named action owner, supporting party, due date, and completion evidence. |
| What may be communicated externally? | Exact language that can be used; never imply endorsement or partnership beyond what is confirmed. |
| What changes in the project? | Specific document, configuration, workflow, risk log, or engineering item to update. |

## 9. Project update trigger

After each stakeholder meeting, update the following controlled project records within two business days:

1. **THE JOURNEY.md** — add a dated milestone only if the meeting created a material commitment, approval, validation result, partner decision, or pilot-design change.
2. **Pilot charter / evidence protocol / backlog** — update only where a recorded decision changes the controlled scope, evidence requirement, owner, or stage gate.
3. **Stakeholder action register** — record all actions, including actions that do not yet justify a narrative milestone.
4. **Git history** — commit material document/configuration changes with a clear message and link the milestone in `THE JOURNEY.md` when appropriate.
