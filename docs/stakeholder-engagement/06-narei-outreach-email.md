Dear NAREI Team,

I am writing on behalf of **Libertas Alpha Technologies** to request NAREI’s collaboration on the initial Guyana pilot of **AgriScore**, a decision-support platform for assessing pre-plant feasibility for **maize and ginger**.

The pilot will organise soil, climate, crop, and market evidence into an explainable feasibility assessment for farmers and extension officers. It is not a lending, insurance, procurement, or contracting system; all assessments will remain subject to qualified human review.

We would value NAREI’s guidance on crop-profile validation, soil-testing requirements, ginger plant-health safeguards, and a practical officer-led evidence-collection workflow.

Could we arrange a brief introductory meeting to present the pilot and discuss a possible technical collaboration?
